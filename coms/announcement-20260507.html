Good afternoon,

We have been made aware of a cyber-related incident affecting Canvas Cloud and are currently investigating the situation.

Instructure, the company behind Canvas Cloud, informed institutions of a systems breach. Information that may have been involved includes names, email addresses, student ID numbers and messages among Canvas users. Instructure reports that there is no evidence that passwords have been compromised.

What you can do

Regularly changing your password is a key action you can take to support the security of your data. As an SFU Continuing Studies student, please follow the steps on the Canvas login page to update your password.

We continue to monitor the situation closely. Please visit ServiceHub for latest updates
